Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Seedline Labs ("we," "us") is based in Sarasota, Florida. This policy explains what information we collect through seedlinelabs.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, mobile number and email when you fill out a form or book a call, plus anything you share with us on a call or by message.
  • Basic website data: like most websites, our hosting and tools may collect standard technical data such as your browser type and the pages you visit.

How we use your information

  • To schedule and hold your free audit call.
  • To contact you about your audit and our services by phone, text or email.
  • If you checked the consent box, to send you calls and text messages, which may include automated calls and texts, at the number you gave us.
  • To keep records of our conversations and improve our service.

Text messages (SMS)

If you agree to receive texts from Seedline Labs, message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Giving consent to receive texts is not required to book a call.

We do not sell or share your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our scheduling tool (Calendly), our CRM (HubSpot), our website host, and our phone and messaging providers. They may use it only to provide their services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to work with you and for our business records, unless you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to opt out of text messages.
  • Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we have about you.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in the email, or by contacting us.
